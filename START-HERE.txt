WHAT CHANGED IN 68
================================================================
1. THE REWARDS ASK THE REWARDS DOOR
   Until now, to show one customer their points, the website downloaded
   the whole business with the secret key: every customer, every sale,
   and a key that could change them. Now it asks the platform's rewards
   door, with the public key and the door's own key, about one customer
   number at a time. The door hands back that customer's points and the
   four rewards figures from Settings, and nothing else: no name, no
   phone, no sale.

   The points are worked out exactly as the till works them out. Checked
   on more than 4,400 test customers, in shops laid out both ways the
   platform keeps its sales: the same points, every one.

2. THE CODE THAT LINKS AN ACCOUNT
   The email on the customer's record is asked of the door only to send
   the code, and is never shown or kept. A number already linked to
   another account is not asked about at all.

3. LIMITS THE WEBSITE CANNOT LIFT
   The door answers at most 1,000 points questions an hour, and 20 emails
   an hour (3 for any one number). They are the database's own limits, so
   even somebody holding the door's key could not copy the customer list
   out. Past them, the rewards say "cannot be reached just now" until the
   hour is up. They never fall back to the old way because of a limit.

4. /api/rewards-check
   Says which way the rewards read the platform, and what to do next.

5. SETTINGS > SHOPPING
   The rewards line now names POS_REWARDS_KEY instead of the secret key.

WHAT CHANGED IN 63
================================================================
1. THE PRODUCT FEED ASKS THE PRODUCTS-ONLY DOOR
   Until then it downloaded the whole business with the secret key, kept
   the products and threw the rest away. Now it asks the platform's door,
   with the public key, and the door hands out only the shop window:
   active pieces, their names, prices, sizes, colours, details and the
   "in stock" answers. Never a cost, a supplier, a customer or a sale.

   If /api/products does not say "door", the platform's products door
   SQL has not been run: tools/vbp-website-door.sql, in the platform's
   build 454 and every build since. Run it in the platform's Supabase
   before STEP 5.

2. THE CHANGE BELL ASKS THE DOOR TOO
   The pulse that tells open pages "something changed" asks the door
   when the products last changed, instead of reading the business with
   the secret key.

3. "I CANNOT READ THE PRODUCTS" IS NO LONGER "THERE ARE NO PRODUCTS"
   If neither the door nor the old way can read the products, the feed
   says so. Pages already open keep showing their pieces instead of going
   empty.

4. CONSENT WHEN CREATING AN ACCOUNT
   The sign-up form has the same kind of consent box as checkout. No
   account is made until it is ticked. Wording: Settings > Customer
   Accounts. When they agreed is kept on the account.
