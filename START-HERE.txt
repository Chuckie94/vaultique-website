================================================================
WHAT CHANGED IN THIS ROUND
================================================================
1. THE PRODUCT FEED ASKS THE PRODUCTS-ONLY DOOR
   Until now it downloaded the whole business with the secret key, kept
   the products and threw the rest away. Now it asks the platform's door,
   with the public key, and the door hands out only the shop window:
   active pieces, their names, prices, sizes, colours, details and the
   "in stock" answers. Never a cost, a supplier, a customer or a sale.

   What a shopper sees is exactly the same, piece for piece and field for
   field. It was checked on more than 4,000 test pieces, including every
   odd way a stock count or a name can be written.

2. THE CHANGE BELL ASKS THE DOOR TOO
   The pulse that tells open pages "something changed" now asks the door
   when the products last changed, instead of reading the business with
   the secret key.

3. "I CANNOT READ THE PRODUCTS" IS NO LONGER "THERE ARE NO PRODUCTS"
   If neither the door nor the old way can read the products, the feed now
   says so. Pages already open keep showing their pieces instead of going
   empty.

Nothing else changed. Rewards, chat, analytics, payments and the admin
are exactly as in build 62.
